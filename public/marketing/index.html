Feature / pull rules of thumb
  • 🆕 New arrival → grid post + "New In" story within 48h.
  • ✅ Back in stock → re-run the reel that sold it out.
  • ⛔ Sold out → pause any live ad pointing to it (don't pay for dead traffic).
  • 🔻 Price drop → a "now AED X" story is your highest-converting format.
  • 📦 Collection updated → refresh the homepage hero + a carousel.

Tone of voice
  • Warm, calm, confident — like a friend with great taste.
  • Speak to the home, not the product spec. "A home to gather in," not "3-seater sofa."
  • Short sentences. Real feelings. No hype, no exclamation storms.
  • Respectful and inclusive for a multi-national UAE audience.
Do & don't
  • ✅ Let the product breathe — generous space, natural light.
  • ✅ Style in real rooms, real moments.
  • ⛔ No cluttered collages or hard-sell price stickers on hero posts.
  • ⛔ Don't stretch or recolor the wordmark.
